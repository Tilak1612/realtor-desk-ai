WHAT IT IS
  Realtor Desk is a bilingual (English/French) CRM for Canadian real estate
  agents, teams and brokerages. It holds leads, contacts, conversations,
  tasks and deals in one place, ranks leads by a 0-100 score, and moves them
  across a pipeline that carries a CAD value per stage.

WHO IT IS FOR
  Licensed Canadian real estate agents working solo or in small teams, and
  brokerages. It is built around Canadian practice: CAD pricing, data hosted
  in a Canadian region, CASL email footers, and French as a first-class
  language rather than a translation layer.

WHAT SHIPS TODAY
  - Lead scoring: intent, urgency, budget and timeline produce a 0-100 score,
    with the contributing factors and a suggested next action on the lead.
  - Pipeline: drag-and-drop stages; each column shows its count and CAD value.
  - Conversation timeline: messages, notes and tasks beside the contact record.
  - Bilingual EN/FR across the app, emails and every lead record; language is
    set per contact.
  - Realtor.ca listing import: paste a listing URL or MLS number.
  - Calendar sync: two-way with Google Calendar and Outlook.
  - Contact import: Google, Microsoft, or CSV upload.
  - Lead capture webhooks: Zapier, Make and n8n; new leads arrive as contacts,
    de-duplicated by email.
  - SMS through the customer's own Twilio account, consent recorded per number.
  - Consent and unsubscribe: consent date and source on every contact; an
    unsubscribe is honoured immediately and permanently.
  - An AI assistant inside the app that can see the contact you have open and
    draft a reply, summarise a thread, or talk through a next step, in English
    or French.
  - Reporting: response time, deals closed, stage conversion, lead source,
    in CAD.

WHAT IS NOT BUILT YET — do not describe these as available
  - CREA DDF(R) sync. The sync function is a scaffold awaiting CREA
    credentials. Targeted Q3 2026. Listing import today is via Realtor.ca.
  - Automated email sequences. The sequence builder exists in the app;
    scheduled sending is not switched on.
  - A lead-facing chatbot on the customer's own website. The AI assistant is
    internal to the CRM and requires sign-in. It does not answer leads
    unattended, read MLS data, or write to a calendar.
  - Team round-robin routing.
  - Granular team roles and an exportable audit log.
  - Market forecasting of any kind. There is no price prediction.

COMPLIANCE — what is accurate to say
  Data is hosted in a Canadian region. Consent date and source are recorded on
  every contact. Outbound email carries a CASL-compliant footer with a working
  unsubscribe, honoured immediately and permanently. The product is built with
  PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 in mind.
  Do NOT state that Realtor Desk holds SOC 2, ISO 27001 or any certification,
  or that it provides FINTRAC workflows — none of these exist.
