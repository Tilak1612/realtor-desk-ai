How to use this sheet

For the agent. Do not put this page out for visitors.

Before the open house

  1. Fill in every line at the top. A request for consent under Canada's anti-spam law has to name who is asking and give a mailing address plus a phone number, an email address or a web address.
  2. Print one sheet for every twelve visitors you expect, and bring a pen that works.

During

  • Signing in is optional, and so is the email box. Do not tick it for anyone.
  • If someone asks you a question about the property, note it. An inquiry is a different thing from a name on a list.

After

  1. Enter the contacts the same day. The matching CSV on the template page has the same columns.
  2. For each person who ticked Yes, record the date and "open house sign-in sheet" as the source of consent.
  3. Do not add anyone who left the box empty to a mailing list.
  4. File the paper sheet. The sender has the onus of proving consent.

Outside Canada

The consent wording follows Canadian law. Other countries have their own rules for email, texts and calls. Check them before you use the sheet there.
